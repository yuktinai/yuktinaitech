This Privacy Policy is intended to support disclosures commonly required for app marketplace listings, including Google Play privacy and data safety disclosures.

3. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information we process when you:

create an account in RYNC; sign in with email and password or a supported identity provider; build or edit your player profile; upload profile photos, achievement images, or community media; use player discovery, challenge, chat, match, notification, badge, and community features; enable optional permissions such as location, notifications, or gallery access; or contact support.

This Privacy Policy does not govern third-party websites, services, or apps that are not operated by us, even if they are linked from our Services.

4. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information from you, from your device, from your activity in the Services, and from third-party providers you choose to use.

Account and identity information: email address, authentication identifiers, session data, sign-in provider details, verification status, and password reset metadata.

Profile and onboarding information: display name, age-related information, city and country, bio, profile photo, sports played, skill levels, years played, availability, search preferences, badges, achievements, and related submissions.

Location information: approximate location, precise device location if permitted, map-selected location, stored coordinates for distance-based matching, and approximate distance information shown to other users.

User content and communications: community posts, comments, reactions, private chat messages, challenge invitations, match messages, recommendations, reviews, uploaded images, and support communications.

Match, challenge, and community activity: challenges sent or received, match scheduling details, outcomes, usage statistics, notification counts, and badge eligibility information.

Device and technical information: device identifiers where applicable, push notification tokens such as Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, IP address, app version, operating system, device model, logs, diagnostics, and API metadata needed for security and reliability.

Permissions-based data access: photos or media, location, and notifications, only when the relevant feature requires it and permission is granted where required.

5. Sources of Information

We collect information directly from you, automatically from your device, from other users interacting with you in the Services, from third-party services you choose to use, and from infrastructure providers that help us operate the Services.
